`Tooltip` adds a short fact on hover: an exact timestamp behind a relative time, or a full name behind a truncated one.

```jsx
<Tooltip label="Last warmed Sep 12, 14:02 ET"><StatusPill tone="ok">1d</StatusPill></Tooltip>
<Tooltip label="Collapsing since Sep 10" side="left">…</Tooltip>
```

Keep it to one line of fact. It never carries instructions.
