Icon

phosphor · fill · currentColor
Family @phosphor-icons/react, re-exported through src/components/ui/icons.tsx — import from that module, never the package, so a weight change is one edit · Weight fill · Colour always currentColor, so an icon inherits its row's state and never carries its own · glyphs drawn here are stand-ins, the rules are the authority
Size
size-3 · 12
size-3.5 · 14
size-4 · 16
12 inline with text · 14 in controls · 16 in the nav icon box
Exceptions — four glyphs fill destroys
CircleNotch · a spinner, not a disc
Caret · bold — a direction, not a wedge
X · two strokes, not a slab
Plus / Minus · marks inside the control
Fill is the house weight everywhere else. These four are stroked or bold because filling them changes what they mean, not just how they look.

09

Rules

the part no token can record
  1. Never hardcode a hex.
  2. One accent use per screen.
  3. Dark is designed. Light is the same markup, flat.
  4. No instruction text on a screen.
  5. Every number is tabular.
  6. Destructive means press and hold.
  7. Glass needs glow.
  8. Controls are pills. Surfaces are 24 out, 16 in.
  9. The limit in the UI is the real limit.
  10. Say what is missing, not that something failed.
  11. No scrollbars — so imply the scroll.
  12. Changelog in the same pass.
