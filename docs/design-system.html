07

Rules

the part no token can record
  1. Never hardcode a hex.
  2. One accent use per screen.
  3. Dark is designed. Light is the same markup, flat.
  4. No instruction text on a screen.
  5. Every number is tabular.
  6. Destructive means press and hold.
  7. Glass needs glow.
  8. Controls are pills. Surfaces are 24 out, 16 in.
  9. The limit in the UI is the real limit.
  10. Say what is missing, not that something failed.
  11. No scrollbars — so imply the scroll.
  12. Changelog in the same pass.
